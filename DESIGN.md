## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the high-performance engineering, pristine atmosphere, and Mediterranean reliability of modern air conditioning commerce. The aesthetic marries architectural minimalism with technical HVAC authority, delivering a reassuringly premium e-commerce experience.

The visual direction addresses two core divisions:
1. **Pristine Brand-New GREE Climate Tier (70%):** Airy, hygienic, precision-crafted layouts characterized by generous white space, deep alpine-forest greens, cool atmospheric tints, and crisp mechanical typography. It evokes purified air, structural silence, and advanced eco-inverter efficiency.
2. **Certified Inspected Second-Hand Outlet (30%):** Grounded, technical, and radically transparent. It leverages dark architectural graphite surfaces, technical inspection badges (A+, A, B), amber certification stamps, and checklist telemetry that signal thorough laboratory testing, safety verification, and honest engineering value.

The resulting tone blends Mediterranean warmth with crisp Nordic-inspired functional minimalism—eliminating cluttered discount-retail patterns in favor of structured luxury and industrial confidence.

## Colors

The color palette sets a deliberate climate-controlled cadence. Rather than generic retail blues, the system centers on deep, architectural forest greens anchored by technical carbon graphite.

- **Primary (`#0F382E`):** Represents certified engineering, stability, and enduring eco-efficiency. Used for master titles, top navigation bars, key purchase actions, and primary interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** A vibrant, botanical climate green signifying energy efficiency (A+++), active cooling states, verified warranties, and the "SIFIR" (Brand New) condition seal.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`):** Precision cooling cyan utilized strictly for thermal indicators, cooling BTU metrics, technical refrigeration diagrams, and air-flow state badges.
- **Neutral (`#1A2120`):** A deep charcoal-graphite with subtle green undertones, preventing cold sterile grays and establishing contrast across high-density spec sheets, inspection reports, and typography.
- **Surface Systems:** The backdrop alternates between `#FFFFFF` for pristine machinery showpieces, `#FBFBF9` for warm Mediterranean architectural warmth, and `#EBF1F0` for refreshing cooling blocks.

Condition labeling utilizes explicit contrast:
- `SIFIR` components wear `#ECFDF5` background with `#065F46` border and typography.
- `İKİNCİ EL` units are framed in dark slate `#1E293B` or technical amber `#D97706` to immediately declare verified pre-owned authenticity without cheapening the product display.

## Typography

The typographic architecture balances approachable high-ticket consumer trust with the precision of HVAC refrigeration blueprints:

- **Primary Voice (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its crisp geometric construction, modern terminal cuts, and approachable humanist posture. It eliminates the sterility of generic enterprise sans-serifs while retaining authoritative weight across headlines, editorial intros, and pricing structures.
- **Engineering Voice (`Space Grotesk`):** Reserved strictly for technical indices, BTU outputs (`9.000 BTU/h`, `12.000 BTU/h`, `24.000 BTU/h`), area requirements (`25-35 m²`), noise ratings (`19 dB(A)`), and certified grade badges (`GRADE A+`). Its slightly mechanical tabular demeanor reinforces mathematical verification and testing precision.

Numerical data must always align using tabular numeral styling (`tnum`) in tables, price rows, and calculator comparison modules to ensure visual balance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured 12-column architectural grid on desktop systems (max content bounding box: `1360px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1024px`) and a 4-column layout on mobile (`< 768px`).

- **Vertical Cadence:** Built on an 8pt base unit with high-volume macro spacing. Premium climate hardware requires wide visual margins to signal high air purity and unobstructed airflow. Hero banners and transition bands use `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` gaps to avoid retail visual congestion.
- **Component Packaging:** Micro modules, metric chips, and filter buttons operate on tight, disciplined intervals (`space-2xs` to `space-sm`) ensuring information density remains crisp, readable, and functional.
- **Responsive Fluidity:** Grids adapt from 4-column product matrices on large displays down to 2-column or single-column detailed spec rows on mobile with sticky horizontal-scroll filters for BTU capacities and condition switches.

## Elevation & Depth

To reflect high-end industrial engineering and hygienic climate equipment, visual depth avoids heavy theatrical drop-shadows. Depth is built through **tonal layering**, **ambient atmospheric tinted diffusion**, and **fine architectural hairline frames**:

1. **Base Tier (Flat / Surface):** `#FBFBF9` or `#FFFFFF` canvas bound by `#E2E8F0` hairline borders (1px solid).
2. **Elevated Cards (Hardware Showcase):** Surfaces sit on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#CFE4E1`) and an ultra-subtle tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 56, 46, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 56, 46, 0.02)`.
3. **Hover & Focus Interactions:** Elevation expands gracefully with soft emerald diffusion: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 56, 46, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.06)`, producing an energetic lift without synthetic black shadow mud.
4. **Floating Action Layer (BTU Picker, WhatsApp Help Bar, Mini-Cart Drawer):** High-priority controls utilize frosted acrylic glassmorphism: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6); box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(17, 22, 21, 0.12)`.
5. **Technical Second-Hand Inspection Modals:** Dark graphite surfaces (`#1A2120`) use deep interior glow containment: `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12); box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a refined **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard. This captures the smooth contouring of modern indoor AC units (which curve gently to direct laminar airflow) while preserving industrial precision:

- Standard controls, inputs, and selection chips: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Product cards, interactive calculator panels, and specification modules: `1rem` (16px).
- Modals, hero highlight panels, and second-hand diagnostic reports: `1.5rem` (24px).
- Technical condition tags, status stamps, and BTU selectors use continuous pill formats (`9999px`) to create clear tactile affordance distinct from structural card containers.
